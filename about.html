Our Story

One commodity.
Full expertise.

Falcon Diamond was built on a simple conviction: the B2B sugar market deserves a trading partner that does nothing else. While generalist commodity traders spread attention across dozens of categories, we chose depth over breadth.

Our focus is deliberately narrow raw, refined, and specialty sugar grades, sourced from Brazil, India, Thailand, and the EU, delivered to buyers and refineries across MENA, South Asia, and East Africa.

That singular focus means our commercial relationships, compliance knowledge, and freight networks are all calibrated to one product. When you trade with Falcon Diamond, you're dealing with a team that eats, breathes, and thinks sugar every day.

What we stand for

Values that drive every trade.

Six principles shaping every decision from the deal desk to the last mile.

Quality Without Compromise

Every grade is tied to defined ICUMSA, polarization, and moisture parameters not rounded estimates. We never trade down on specification.

Supply Reliability

Diversified origin portfolios and committed mill relationships keep your supply pipeline intact even when one market is disrupted by weather or policy.

Transparent Pricing

Our offers include a clear price build-up: origin cost, freight, inspection, and margin no opaque bundling. You see what drives the number.

Partnership Mindset

We build for recurring relationships, not one-off transactions. Contract structures call-offs, indexed pricing, fixed windows are designed to benefit long-term buyers.

Documentation Excellence

Full document packages SGS/BV certificates, B/L, phytosanitary, COO, packing lists prepared to import-authority standards before vessels sail.

Trade on your terms.

Every time.
